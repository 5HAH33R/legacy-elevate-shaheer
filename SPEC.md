# Elevate — Product Specification

## 1. Overview

Elevate is an AI-powered business management platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) in Pakistan. It brings essential business operations into one simple, modern workspace.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many small businesses manage sales, inventory, expenses, invoices, and supplier information using separate registers, spreadsheets, or applications. This makes it difficult to maintain accurate records, monitor stock levels, understand profitability, and make informed business decisions.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Elevate provides a centralized business dashboard that helps business owners manage daily operations, track performance, and use AI-powered assistance to understand their business data.

## 4. Target Users

- Small and medium-sized business owners
- Retail shops and wholesalers
- Inventory and sales managers
- Accountants and finance staff
- Businesses looking to simplify daily operations

## 5. Core Features

- Business registration and workspace setup
- Dashboard showing sales, expenses, inventory, and business summaries
- Product and inventory management
- Stock movement tracking and low-stock alerts
- Customer and supplier management
- Sales records and invoice generation
- Expense tracking and estimated profit reports
- Role-based staff access and permissions
- AI business assistant supporting English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu
- AI-generated marketing content and business suggestions
- Light and dark themes
- Exportable reports

## 6. AI Capabilities

The AI assistant will answer business questions using data the signed-in user is authorized to access. It may summarize trends, explain reports, suggest stock-reordering actions, and draft marketing content.

AI-generated suggestions should be reviewable, and important business changes must require user confirmation. Financial totals and stock balances must be calculated by application logic rather than generated by AI.

## 7. Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Next.js server-side logic and API Route Handlers
- **Database:** Cloud Firestore
- **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication
- **File storage:** Cloud Storage for Firebase
- **AI integration:** An AI API accessed securely from the server
- **Hosting:** A hosting platform compatible with Next.js

## 8. User Roles

- **Business Owner:** Manages the workspace, team, and business records
- **Manager:** Handles assigned operational tasks
- **Accountant:** Manages authorized invoices, payments, and expenses
- **Inventory Manager:** Manages products and stock
- **Sales Staff:** Handles permitted customer and sales workflows
- **Platform Administrator:** Manages platform operations without routine access to private business financial records

Permissions must be enforced on the server, not only by hiding interface elements.

## 9. Security and Privacy

- Authenticate users before allowing access to private business data.
- Enforce role-based permissions on the server.
- Isolate each business's records from other businesses.
- Keep API credentials and secrets on the server.
- Validate user inputs and important changes.
- Maintain audit logs for sensitive operations.
- Minimize private data sent to external AI services.

## 10. Design and Branding

Elevate uses a modern, high-contrast visual identity.

- **Electric Lime:** `#C6FF00`
- **Carbon Black:** `#080A08`
- **Graphite:** `#171B16`
- **Soft White:** `#F6F8F4`

The interface should be responsive, accessible, and available in light and dark modes.

## 11. MVP Scope

The initial version will focus on:

1. Authentication and business workspace setup
2. Staff roles and permissions
3. Products and inventory
4. Customers and suppliers
5. Sales records and invoices
6. Expense tracking
7. Dashboard and basic reports
8. A limited AI business assistant

Advanced forecasting, social media publishing, community features, and additional integrations may be introduced in later phases.

## 12. Success Criteria

- A business owner can create and configure a workspace.
- Authorized staff can manage their assigned operations.
- Users can record sales, generate invoices, and track inventory.
- Dashboards and reports reflect validated business records.
- AI responses respect user permissions and communicate relevant limitations.
- One business cannot access another business's private records.

## 13. Project Principle

Build a secure, reliable, and easy-to-use business platform that helps Pakistani SMEs save time, improve record keeping, and make better-informed business decisions.

## 14. Repository Scope Before Kickoff

Until the project kickoff, this repository may contain documentation only. Application source code and implementation files should be added after kickoff, in accordance with the project rules.
